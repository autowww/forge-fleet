# PoC — Proof of Concept

Time-boxed validation slice — often packaged as a Product Spark type, not a Forge Spark task label.


## What it is

Product-level PoC in planning/PLANNING-FLOW.md. Distinct from exploration/discipline spike.

## When people say this

When scoping earliest shippable learning for investors or Assay Gate evidence.

## Where it lives

blueprints/sdlc/methodologies/forge/planning/

## How it fits the ecosystem

Aliases: release slice, product increment for Product Spark family.

## Typical usage in plans and chat

Do not label a learning spike as PoC if it is spike_discipline work — see DISCIPLINE-SPIKE.md.

## Do not confuse with

MVP
Product-Spark
