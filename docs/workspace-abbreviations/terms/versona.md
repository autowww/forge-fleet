# Versona — Versona (discipline virtual persona)

Bounded discipline reviewer with optional §5 structured concern report — templates in bp versona/catalog/.


## What it is

Installed to .cursor/rules/versona-*.mdc via sync-forge-cursor-rules.sh from forge.config.yaml.

## When people say this

When invoking challenge pass, roadmap gate, or discipline-specific Cursor reviews.

## Where it lives

blueprints/sdlc/methodologies/forge/versona/

## How it fits the ecosystem

Versona session folders under forge-logs/versona/<actor>/<session-id>/ — not team meetings.

## Typical usage in plans and chat

Suggest next Versonas in §5 output; do not substitute Versona-only for human sign-off on binding decisions.
